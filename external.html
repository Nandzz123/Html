BSc Physics

BSc Physics is an undergraduate degree of 3 years. It teaches students about Physics and its elements. The BSc Physics Syllabus teaches students the study of matter, energy, light, motion, space, and time. It also puzzles out how and why these interact with one another.

BSc chemistry

A BSc in Chemistry is a 3-year-long UG course offering both theoretical and practical knowledge of Chemistry and its different disciplines. The BSc Chemistry syllabus is divided into 6 semesters revolving around Chemical properties, compositions, organic, inorganic and physical chemistry..

BA English

BA in English Literature is a 3 years UG course which deals with the study of literature in English. This course is all about explaining the various kinds of literary works such as poems, novels, plays, and more. This BA in English Literature course provides you the literature, language, and interdisciplinary knowledge.

BA History

BA History BA or Bachelor of Arts in History is a three-year undergraduate course. A BA History course covers various aspects of History, such as cultural, social, economic, political and ideological, to provide a better understanding of the subject to candidates.
